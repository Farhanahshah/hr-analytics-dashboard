HR Analytics Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard on employee attrition, salary, department performance and demographics.

## Business question
Why are employees leaving, and which groups are most affected?

## Tools
Power BI, Power Query, DAX

## What I did
1. Cleaned and transformed the HR dataset in Power Query.
2. Created DAX measures for key workforce KPIs.
3. Designed a dashboard covering attrition, salary, department performance and demographics.
